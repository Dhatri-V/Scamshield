Common Scam Categories

Recognise the patterns behind everyday digital scams.

Phishing

Fake emails or messages that try to steal passwords or financial details.

Fake Jobs

Fraudulent offers that ask for fees, personal documents, or urgent action.

Impersonation

Scammers pretending to be banks, delivery services, officials, or relatives.

OTP and Payment Fraud

Pressure tactics designed to trick users into sharing codes or sending money.

  • Everyday users unsure about suspicious links and messages

Safety Tips

Simple habits can prevent many scams.

Slow down urgent messages

Scammers often push for immediate action so people react before thinking.

Check the sender carefully

Look for misspelled names, unusual addresses, and unfamiliar numbers.

Never share codes casually

OTP codes, passwords, and banking details should not be shared in a rush.

Verify on official channels

Visit the official website directly instead of trusting a link in a message.
